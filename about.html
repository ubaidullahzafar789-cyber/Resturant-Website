Since 2009

A Restaurant Born From Passion

Saveur began as a small family kitchen with a big idea: that great food comes from great ingredients, treated with respect. What started as a twelve-seat bistro on Maple Avenue has grown into one of the region's most beloved dining destinations — without ever losing that original spirit.

Our founder, Chef Antoine Laurent, believed that a meal should feel like a conversation: honest, surprising, and unhurried. Fifteen years later, every plate that leaves our kitchen still carries that philosophy.

We grow relationships with the farmers, fishers, and artisans who supply us, because we believe the best cooking starts long before the stove is lit.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

The principles that guide every decision in our kitchen and dining room.

Our Mission

To create memorable dining experiences through seasonal, honest cooking — supporting local producers while delighting every guest who walks through our doors.

Our Vision

To be the restaurant that defines fine dining in our region — proving that elegance and warmth, luxury and accessibility, can sit at the same table.

Meet the Chef

Chef Antoine Laurent

With over twenty years behind the stove, Chef Antoine brings a rare blend of classical training and fearless curiosity. After apprenticing in Lyon and leading kitchens across Europe, he returned home to open Saveur — a place where technique serves flavor, never the other way around.

His menus change with the seasons, but his philosophy doesn't: cook what's ripe, source what's nearby, and never rush a good thing.

"A great plate isn't about showing off. It's about making someone feel something they didn't expect."

Chef Antoine Laurent
